# UX and marketing practice

In workspace 03, Acquisition explicitly separates Sample and GA4 modes. Sample counts are invented; unconfigured GA4 mode shows no results. Use the sample to learn metric scopes, campaign naming, segmentation, denominators and cautious interpretation. Live evidence requires validated production collection and reporting authorization. Beta remains excluded. Earlier Acquisition previews remain exercises only.

## A manageable working cycle

1. Pick one decision: for example, whether an external community post helps people find useful regional rankings.
2. Define the audience, campaign, landing page and outcome before publishing. A ranking-filter action is observable behavior, not proof of satisfaction; region submission is useful only when it matches the campaign's purpose.
3. Write a measurement plan: event, trigger, session/browser scope, numerator, denominator, observation period, exclusions and privacy limits. Verify initial page/content views, campaign fields and duplicate prevention before interpreting the report.
4. Publish one deliberately tagged external link after collection is ready. Keep lowercase source, medium and campaign names consistent. Do not tag internal navigation or include personal information.
5. Review volume and outcome rate together. Compare like-for-like devices and landing pages. Keep unknowns visible. Do not treat a few high-rate visits as a winning channel or compare first-acquisition identity rates directly to session rates.
6. Record one observation, plausible alternatives, a proposed change and the next measurement date. Combine behavior with optional asynchronous feedback where needed. A visitor leaving a page does not establish confusion.
7. Make a small relevant change and review it. Before/after differences can reflect seasonality, traffic mix or releases; causal claims require a valid experiment. Low traffic may mean the honest result is inconclusive.

The public collector now preserves bounded, complete external-entry campaign fields explicitly while keeping arbitrary queries out of page locations. This is local code, not deployed or account-verified attribution. Before publishing links for measurement, confirm consent/collection settings and validate attribution in GA4. The earlier preview's source labels do not reproduce GA4 non-direct or multi-touch processing; workspace 03's configured reporting path requests GA4's session-source dimensions rather than reclassifying them itself.

## What to retain as experience

Keep a short case-study record: question, measurement specification, tracking QA, baseline, tagged campaign or design change, analysis with exact denominators, limitations, decision and follow-up. Report genuine work and outcomes honestly; label simulated exercises separately. Real practice can begin with organic/community channels and does not require paid ads or moderated interviews.

## Suggested first sequence

- Acquisition: identify where visits come from and what activity follows.
- Features and journeys: investigate discovery and steps within the product.
- Feedback: use optional asynchronous comments to investigate possible explanations.
- Retention: review returning cohorts after enough history exists.
- Experiments: only launch after stable assignment/exposure tracking and a feasible sample plan exist.
